# NZ politics — current state

**As of:** 2026-11-27 (official final results of the 7 Nov 2026 general election released)

## Parliament (final official count, 130 seats, 66 for a majority)

| Party | Seats |
|---|---|
| National | 40 |
| Labour | 36 |
| Greens | 17 |
| ACT | 13 |
| NZ First | 12 |
| Opportunity (TOP) | 7 |
| Te Pāti Māori | 5 |

Party vote: Labour 29.88%, National 28.35% (The Spinoff).

Key facts:
- National + ACT + NZ First = 65, one short. They had 67 on election night;
  two National electorates flipped on specials, including Wairarapa to Labour by 81 votes.
- Labour + Greens + Te Pāti Māori = 58; with Opportunity = 65, also one short.
- Opportunity (leader Qiulae Wong) holds the balance and has not ruled out
  sending voters back to the polls if its conditions aren't met.
- A National recount request in Wairarapa is likely. A flip back would give the
  incumbent bloc 66 on its own.
